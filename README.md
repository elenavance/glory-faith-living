# GLORY — Faith & Living

A premium, mobile-first Christian spiritual companion for Scripture, prayer, biblical wisdom, and everyday faith.

## Vision
Help people grow closer to God through Scripture, prayer, reflection, and practical biblical wisdom. The product welcomes Christians across denominations while clearly distinguishing shared Christian content from tradition-specific practices.

## Visual direction
- Extravagant, luxurious, heavenly, God-centered art direction
- Midnight navy, antique gold, warm ivory, divine-light photography
- Elegant typography, layered atmospheric visuals, restrained motion
- Mobile-first responsive layouts; meaningful content must remain visible even when animations or external assets fail

## Planned experience
- Daily Word: Scripture, explanation, reflection, and application
- Prayer & Devotion: morning/evening prayer, gratitude, intentions, optional audio and reminders
- Biblical Wisdom: relationships, family, work, finances, hardship, forgiveness, growth
- Ask for Guidance: Scripture-informed reflection with appropriate boundaries
- Christian Calendar & Teachings
- My Faith Journey: saved verses, reflections, prayer history, milestones

## Product safeguards
- Do not imply the app replaces clergy, medical care, therapy, or financial professionals.
- Respect differences among Christian traditions.
- Keep notification, audio, and reminder preferences explicit and user-controlled.
- Keep private reflections private.
- Use properly sourced/licensed Scripture translations and media assets.

## Technology direction
Django + PostgreSQL-ready backend, Progressive Web App (PWA), and Railway deployment when the account's resource capacity allows it.

## Current stage
Phase 1: product foundation and visual prototype.
